## Interpretation rule

Morphotypes encode topology and major curvature states, not pixel-perfect endpoints. In photographs, a vein can fade before the visible wing margin because of focus, contrast, damage, mounting or illumination. Treat branch presence/absence, shared junctions, convergence/fusion and relative curvature as stronger evidence than a few missing terminal pixels.
